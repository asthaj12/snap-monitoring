📋 Story 9 — The Courage to Do Nothing

SNAP Eligibility Policy Change — Wave-by-Wave Test & Control Monitor

Federal SNAP eligibility rules changed, affecting 9,293 products across 9 live states. The organization expected significant demand transfer and pushed for repositioning. This dashboard documents how test-control measurement across 7 waves proved demand held flat — and why the right call was to hold the baseline.

Methodology Note
"The recommendation to hold baseline was uncomfortable because SNAP represents 14–17% of Walmart grocery revenue. But acting without evidence would have created the supply chain disruption we were trying to prevent. The test-control design made the finding undeniable — seven waves, nine states, nine thousand products, all consistent. Pre-defined trigger thresholds were armed and monitored every week. They never fired."

The Math Behind the "$50M/Month Avoided" Number
150–200M affected units/month × 5% blanket reaction rate × $3–5 average unit retail
= $22M–$50M/month in unnecessary repositioning cost.

The test-control design replaced a $50M/month supply chain reaction with a $0 cost "hold" decision backed by 9,293-product, 9-state, 7-wave evidence. Pre-defined triggers remained armed throughout. None fired.
